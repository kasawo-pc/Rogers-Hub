Frequently asked questions.

What kind of websites can you build?

Rogers Hub lists custom business websites, portfolios, community platforms, e-commerce storefronts and website redesigns among its services.

Will my website work on phones?

Responsive design and mobile optimization are part of the listed service and package features.

How much does a website cost?

Published starting ranges are UGX 150,000–300,000 for a Starter Portfolio and UGX 300,000–500,000 for Business Website or E-commerce Store packages. Final pricing depends on scope.

How do I request a quote?

Use the contact page, email Rogers or start a WhatsApp conversation with a short description of what you want to build.

Can I request an existing website redesign?

Yes. Mobile optimization and redesign are listed among the services. Share your current website and the improvements you need.

How do I join the community?

Visit the Join Hub page or send a WhatsApp message with your name and interests to ask about joining the community.
