Who I am

Hi, I’m Rogers.

I am a web developer and designer focused on turning ideas into clean, useful and responsive digital experiences. I work with individuals, businesses, clubs and community organizations in Uganda and beyond.

My approach combines thoughtful design, accessible structure, practical functionality and clear communication from the first conversation through handover.

Mission & vision

Technology that helps people move forward.

My mission is to make useful web experiences accessible to local businesses and communities: sites that explain what you do, help people find you and make the next step easy.

I aim to keep learning, build with care and support a stronger local technology community.
