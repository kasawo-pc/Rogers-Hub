Privacy Policy Statement

Rogers Hub respects the privacy of people who visit this website or contact Rogers about services. This policy explains the limited information that may be shared when you choose to get in touch.

1. Information you provide

If you contact Rogers by email, phone or WhatsApp, you may share your name, contact details, project description, images or other information needed to discuss your request. The contact and join forms on this static website prepare a message and open WhatsApp; they do not submit information to a Rogers Hub server.

2. How information is used

Information you choose to send may be used to respond to enquiries, discuss project scope, prepare estimates, provide agreed services and communicate about delivery.

3. Storage and third-party services

Email and WhatsApp are third-party communication services with their own privacy practices and terms. Information sent through those services is handled according to the relevant provider's policies. This website does not currently operate a server-side customer database or newsletter subscription.

4. Sharing and security

Rogers Hub does not sell personal contact information. Information may be shared with a service provider only where needed to deliver a requested service or where required by law. No online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

5. Your choices

You can choose what information to include in a message. Avoid sending passwords, payment-card details or other highly sensitive information through general enquiry channels.

6. Contact

For questions about this policy or information you have shared, contact rogerrichardpark744@gmail.com or call +256 741 798579.
